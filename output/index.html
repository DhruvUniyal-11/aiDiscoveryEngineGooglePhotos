📌 Executive Summary & Core Research Discoveries

This engine analyzes public user evidence to scope the vague-memory retrieval funnel in Google Photos. It operates under a strict non-solutioning requirement (producing discovery intelligence, not feature specs).

Funnel Concentration Locus

Failures concentrate at Stage 2 (System Understanding) and Stage 6 (Abandonment). Users retain natural setting keywords ('Goa café', 'sitting outside'), but standard search parsers fail to map non-standard descriptors to index tags.

Clue Retention Asymmetry

Users remember Place/Setting (82%), Relative Time (78%), and Visual Attributes (65%), while forgetting exact dates, commercial place names, album titles, and face tag names.

📐 Official Retrieval Failure Taxonomy (7 Stages & 12 Categories)
